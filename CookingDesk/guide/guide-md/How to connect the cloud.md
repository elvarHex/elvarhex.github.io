Make sure you have installed the mobile version on your device; you will need it to create your own recipes and discover new ones.



If you haven't used the cloud with CookingDesk yet:

1\. Log in to or sign up for jsonbin.io.

2\. Go to the "API Keys" section and click "Create Access Key".

3\. Assign a name of your choice, grant all permissions for your bins (Create, Read, Update, and Delete), and click "Save Access Key".

4\. You will find the newly created key in the "X-Access-Key" list. Copy the entire key.

5\. Open CookingDesk on your smartphone and go to Settings > General.

6\. Click "Connect" under "JSONBin", paste your key into the "API Key" field, and check the boxes for "Create Index" and "Create Saved-recipes bin".

7\. Click "Connect" to finalize the connection.



If you have already used the cloud with CookingDesk:

1\. Open CookingDesk on your device and go to the "General" settings.

2\. The data listed under JSONBin (API Key, Index Bin, and Saved Bin) represents the location of your data in the cloud. To enter these details automatically on your device, click "Export" and download the configuration file.

3\. On the new device, open CookingDesk and go to the "General" settings section.

4\. Click "Import" and select the file downloaded from the previous device.

5\. You will receive a message confirming that the data has been successfully loaded.



NOTE: If you receive error code "401", it means your API key is incorrect. Please double-check it.
