❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is CookingDesk free to use?

Yes, CookingDesk is 100% free and open-source. You can inspect the source code or contribute.

How does cloud synchronization work?

CookingDesk uses jsonbin.io to securely store and sync your recipes across devices using your API key.

Can I use CookingDesk on mobile devices and in the kitchen?

Yes! The Mobile app is optimized for creating and discovering recipes on the go, while the Desk version offers a step-by-step cooking interface for when you are at the stove.
